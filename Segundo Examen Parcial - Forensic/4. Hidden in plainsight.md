## Descripción
You're given a seemingly ordinary JPG image. Something is tucked away out of sight inside the file. Your task is to discover the hidden payload and extract the flag.
Download the jpg image [here](https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/364c5c391edaf4ab3da852e0fc51a5285bc4a570900618b112c52805fc1d5c14/img.jpg).

pistas:
1. Download the jpg image and read its metadata
## Solución
```
- Descargamos la imagen
wget https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/364c5c391edaf4ab3da852e0fc51a5285bc4a570900618b112c52805fc1d5c14/img.jpg

- Leemos metadatos: el campo Comment trae base64
exiftool img.jpg
Comment : c3RlZ2hpZGU6Y0VGNmVuZHZjbVE9

- decodificamos -> "steghide:cEF6endvcmQ=", y el segundo base64 -> "pAzzword"
echo c3RlZ2hpZGU6Y0VGNmVuZHZjbVE9 | base64 -d
echo cEF6endvcmQ= | base64 -d

- Entonces se usó steghide con la contraseña pAzzword; extraemos lo oculto
steghide extract -sf img.jpg -p pAzzword
cat flag.txt

- encontramos la flag:
academy{h1dd3n_1n_1m4g3_45cfe9c1}
```
