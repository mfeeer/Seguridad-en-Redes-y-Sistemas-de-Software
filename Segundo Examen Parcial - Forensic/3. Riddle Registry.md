## Descripción
You've stumbled upon a peculiar PDF filled with what seems like nothing more than garbled nonsense. But beware! Not everything is as it appears. Amidst the chaos lies a hidden treasure—an elusive flag waiting to be uncovered.
Find the PDF file here [Hidden Confidential Document](https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/b32f1f1945481591df61d328d02995fe3521d16b70928fbe44e3bd90c4ccb113/confidential.pdf) and uncover the flag within the metadata.

pistas:
1. Don't be fooled by the visible text; it's just a decoy!
2. Look beyond the surface for hidden clues
## Solución
```
- Descargamos el PDF
wget https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/b32f1f1945481591df61d328d02995fe3521d16b70928fbe44e3bd90c4ccb113/confidential.pdf

- Revisamos los metadatos; el campo Author tiene una cadena en base64
exiftool confidential.pdf
Author : YWNhZGVteXtwdXp6bDNkX20zdGFkYXRhX2YwdW5kIV85ZDNjYzY2OX0=

- decodificamos
echo "YWNhZGVteXtwdXp6bDNkX20zdGFkYXRhX2YwdW5kIV85ZDNjYzY2OX0=" | base64 -d

- encontramos la flag:
academy{puzzl3d_m3tadata_f0und!_9d3cc669}
```
