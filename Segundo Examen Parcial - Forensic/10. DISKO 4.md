## Descripción
Can you find the flag in this disk image? This time I deleted the file! Let see you get it now!
Download the disk image [here](https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/d11cb680ea12a21fc0d5fdb414a325b8c800bd2fbf4b546df09d428f0e84b4c4/disko-4.dd.gz).

pistas:
1. How would you look for deleted files?
## Solución
```
- Descargamos y descomprimimos (sistema de archivos FAT32)
wget https://challenge-files.cylabacademy.net/library/d11cb680ea12a21fc0d5fdb414a325b8c800bd2fbf4b546df09d428f0e84b4c4/disko-4.dd.gz
gunzip disko-4.dd.gz
fsstat disko-4.dd | head    -> FAT32

- Listamos solo los archivos borrados (-d) de forma recursiva
fls -r -d -p disko-4.dd
r/r * 522629:	log/messages
r/r * 532021:	log/dont-delete.gz

- Recuperamos el archivo borrado con icat (por su inodo) y lo descomprimimos
icat disko-4.dd 532021 > dont-delete.gz
zcat dont-delete.gz
Here is your flag
academy{d3l_d0n7_h1d3_w3ll_187a1210}
```
